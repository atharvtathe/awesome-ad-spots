> Where to advertise your SaaS, startup or side project: a curated list of **websites people actually use** (communities, tools, docs, design, security and tech blogs) that sell ad spots directly, with placements, prices and traffic.

**Scope:** websites with their own audience that sell on-site ad placements (banners, sidebars, sponsored posts, in-feed ads, logo placements). Not included: directories, listing sites and launch platforms, newsletter-only and podcast-only sponsorships.

## Related

**Ad networks.** One booking places your ad across many sites like the ones above.

- [EthicalAds](https://www.ethicalads.io/advertisers/) - Privacy-first ads on hand-picked developer sites. 35M+ impressions/mo, CPM $3.10–$8.00, $1,000 minimum.
- [Carbon Ads](https://www.carbonads.net/) - Native ads on 350+ tech and design sites. Typical start ~$5,000/mo.

**Selling ad spots on your own site?** These tools add a "buy an ad" flow to any website.

- [SponsorApp](https://www.producthunt.com/products/sponsorapp) - Adds a "Sponsor" button so visitors can design and buy ad spots directly.
- [CheerAd](https://cheerad.com) - Visitors buy "Spotlight Spots", paid straight to your Stripe, with no tracking.

**Need a way to get paid first?**

- [Awesome Payment Gateways](https://github.com/atharvtathe/awesome-payment-gateways) - Merchant of record platforms, payment gateways and local options, with fees and the countries you can sell from.

## Contributing

Know a website that sells ad spots? Run one? Pull requests are welcome. Please read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) first, or [suggest a site](../../issues/new/choose) without writing any markdown.

Ground rules:

1. The site must have its own users (a community, tool, app, blog or docs site). No directories or listing sites.
2. On-site placements only (no newsletter-only or podcast-only listings).
3. The site must have a public advertise page or a clear way to book. Link to that page.
4. Use the site's own numbers and put the month you checked them in **Verified**.
